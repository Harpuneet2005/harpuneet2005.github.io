ChromaFlash

Making material differences visible

Computational Health & Interaction Laboratory · Toronto, Canada

Built a programmable LED flash that adapts illumination to improve optical contrast between materials, under Prof. Alex Mariakakis. The work explored spectral imaging with ubiquitous cameras.

Characterized 50+ materials across 400–900 nm. Designed high-speed PCBs in Altium with 7 ns LED switching and implemented per-channel brightness and timing control on a Raspberry Pi Pico 2.
